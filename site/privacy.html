Privacy and cookies

What deb.griffo.io and its apt repository collect, why, and what you can do about it

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short. No cookie is set on this site unless you accept Google Analytics, and you can change that choice at any time. If you start a trial or subscribe, the repository keeps what it needs to give you apt access: your email address, your subscription status and a hash of your password. Card details go to Stripe and never reach this site.

1. Who runs this site

deb.griffo.io, the homepage of deb-free.griffo.io and the apt repositories behind them are run by Dario Griffo ("I", "me"). Questions and requests about your data can be raised via GitHub or the project Discord community.

2. Analytics

Google Analytics, only if you accept. Until you click Accept in the cookie banner, no Google script is loaded, no Google cookie is set and nothing is sent from your browser to Google. If you accept, Google Analytics records the pages you visit and a few actions on them: opening the free-trial form, starting a trial and going to checkout. The same measurements are used to judge whether advertising for this site works, so accepting also lets Google connect a trial or a purchase to an ad you clicked; ad personalisation stays off.

Completed purchases. When a payment goes through, the repository's server reports the sale to Google Analytics: the plan, the amount, the currency and Stripe's reference for the checkout. It carries no name and no email address. It is linked to your visit only if you had accepted analytics cookies.

Ahrefs Web Analytics. Pages on deb.griffo.io also load Ahrefs Web Analytics, which works without cookies and reports page statistics in aggregate.

3. Free trial

To start a trial you enter an email address. The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which is loaded only when you open the form; the server checks its answer with Cloudflare, passing along your IP address. The repository stores your email address and, only if you accepted analytics cookies, the Google Analytics client ID from the _ga cookie, so that a purchase made later can be credited to the visit that started the trial. The email address is used to send your credentials, a reminder before the trial ends, and other messages about your access.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Card details go to Stripe and never reach this site or its servers; your use of Stripe is subject to its own terms and privacy policy. The repository keeps your email address, Stripe's references for you and your subscription, the plan, its status and its dates. That email address receives your credentials and messages about your subscription, including one request for a review a couple of days after it starts.

5. Repository access

apt authenticates with your login (your email address) and a password generated for you, which is stored only as a bcrypt hash. Like any web server, the repository's servers log requests, including the IP address, the user agent and the file requested, to keep the service running and to prevent abuse.

6. Cookies and browser storage

NameKindPurpose
_gaCookie, Google AnalyticsTells visits from the same browser apart. Only set after you accept.

dgi-consentLocal storage, this siteRemembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies.

This site sets no other cookies. The two local-storage entries never leave your browser.

7. Your choices

  • Change your cookie choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. Rejecting deletes the Google Analytics cookies.
  • Ask what is held about you, or have it corrected or deleted, via GitHub or the Discord community. GitHub issues are public, so do not post your email address there; say you have a privacy request and it can continue privately. Deleting your account ends your repository access.

8. Changes

If this page changes, the new version is posted here with an updated date.
