❓ PPA questions, answered

Is this a PPA?

Technically no. It's a plain signed APT repository, which is what a PPA is under the hood. You get the same workflow (apt install, apt upgrade) without Launchpad, and unlike a PPA it serves Debian and Ubuntu derivatives too.

Is there an official Spotifast PPA?

The Spotifast project does not maintain an official PPA. Community PPAs come and go and often lag behind; this repository is updated automatically within hours of each official release, and the packaging is public.

Is this repository trustworthy?

It's an unofficial repository maintained by Dario Griffo. Packages are built automatically from official upstream Spotifast releases, the packaging is open source at dariogriffo/spotifast-debian, and the whole archive is GPG-signed. Several upstream projects link to it from their official installation docs.

How fast do new releases arrive?

Typically within hours of the upstream release, usually faster than community PPAs, and years ahead of the frozen Ubuntu archive. Follow the release feed to watch updates land.
